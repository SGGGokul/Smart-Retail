RetailIQ Store Operating Procedures (SOP-2026)

Section 1: Customer Returns & Exchanges
Items may be returned within 30 days of purchase with a valid original receipt at the Customer Service Desk.
Perishable dairy, fresh meat, and produce items must be returned within 48 hours of purchase.
Refunds are issued to the original form of payment. Unopened non-perishable goods without receipt qualify for store merchandise credit.

Section 2: Slip and Fall Safety Protocol
Upon detecting any liquid spill or glass breakage in aisles, employees must place high-visibility yellow caution cones within 60 seconds.
Never leave an active wet spill unattended. Contact store facilities via channel 2 on the store radio for dry mop cleanup.

Section 3: Checkout Register Queue Escalation
Customer queues are monitored by computer-vision cameras over registers 1 through 6.
When any lane experiences a queue of more than 4 waiting customers, or average wait time exceeds 3 minutes, the front-end manager must immediately open a secondary backup register lane.
Lane 3 is equipped for priority express checkout (10 items or fewer).
